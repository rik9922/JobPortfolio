## 1. Creative & Visual Direction: *"The Sculptural Atelier of Software Craft"*

Rather than a conventional resume template or a technical monitoring dashboard, this portfolio is architected as a **five-chapter spatial 3D journey**. Inspired by award-winning creative WebGL showcases (Lusion, Active Theory, Monolith Studio), each chapter unfolds around a distinct **sculptural 3D installation** rendered in real time with **Three.js**, **React Three Fiber**, **Drei `MeshTransmissionMaterial`**, **studio `Lightformer` illumination**, **custom GLSL shaders**, and **GSAP `ScrollTrigger` camera choreography**.

### Aesthetic Foundations
- **Gallery-Grade Editorial Typography**: Pairings of **Syne** (geometric architectural display), **Instrument Serif** (editorial italic counterpoints), and **Plus Jakarta Sans** (clean body copy) over an open, uncluttered viewport.
- **Physical 3D Materials & Studio Lighting**: Refractive optical glass (`MeshTransmissionMaterial`), brushed champagne titanium, polished obsidian, and custom GLSL iridescence under warm champagne (`#F3E5AB`), electric cobalt (`#3B82F6`), and amber coral (`#F97316`) studio lightformers.
- **Interactive 3D Storytelling**: Every technical capability is represented through a reactive 3D diorama that morphs geometry, optical planes, and data conduits when exploring capability domains and perspective lenses.
